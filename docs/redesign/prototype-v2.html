Why this matters to you

There is no virus to find. The attacker used normal sign-ins and normal app permissions. Your job is to spot legitimate features being misused, which is the hardest kind of attack to see.

What we know
Entry was a legacy, non-production test tenant account with no MFA.
The attacker used residential proxies and a low number of attempts to avoid detection.
A small percentage of corporate mailboxes were accessed, including senior leadership, cybersecurity and legal.
They searched for information about Midnight Blizzard itself: what Microsoft knew about them.

Field glossary
Password spray

Try a few common passwords against many accounts, slowly, so no single account gets locked out.

OAuth app

An app registered in Entra ID that can act with its own permissions, without a user signing in.

Consent

Approval that lets an app use permissions. An admin's consent can unlock access across the company.

full_access_as_app

An Exchange Online app permission that allows access to mailboxes.
